## What the app now does because of them

- **Membership form** mirrors CTFDA's: contact, emergency contact, demographics, involvement, Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment acceptance (required for competitive play, as on the form), waiver, U18 guardian note, fees, and the payment reference rule (CT number, or `member_surname`).
- **Readiness (Road to Nationals):** flags captains and spirit captains without Advanced accreditation, and shows the team's Advanced count against the 50% needed at Nationals.
- **AGM/SGM notice:** warns when a general meeting is set less than 14 days away (21 if a special resolution is proposed), per constitution 7d. Email notices count as received after 4 days (11c).
- **Guide:** "Something happened: harassment, safety or conduct" follows the Anti-Harassment Policy's informal and formal routes, confidentiality and 7-day appeal. These reports deliberately do **not** go through the app's bug inbox.
- **Guide:** "What your team needs for Nationals" summarises Road to Nationals; the document is linked under Rules & accreditation.
- **Gender:** the app uses the Ultimate Rules gender *matching* categories (women/man matching), as the Inclusion Guidelines ask, and never asks for sex assigned at birth.

## Open questions and recommendations

1. **Population group is special personal information (POPIA s26).** CTFDA collects it for growth and diversity tracking. The app stores it privately (the person and admins only), shows admins **totals only**, and leaves it out of exports. It also offers "Prefer not to say", which CTFDA's form doesn't. **Ask CTFDA:** is "Prefer not to say" OK, and is their purpose statement enough consent?
2. **Register of members (constitution 3e)** must show name, **address**, **date membership started** and **date it ended**. The app records submission dates but only asks for suburb, and has no "membership ended" date. **Ask CTFDA:** is suburb enough, or should the app add a full address and a cessation date?
3. **Members may inspect the register (8c)**, but POPIA limits who sees personal details. Suggest: members can see *who* is a member (name, number), never contact details or demographics. **Needs a committee decision.**
4. **"Do not publicly announce or question a player's gender identity" (Inclusion Guidelines).** The app shows gender matching on rosters and line-ups because mixed ratio needs it, but also on public player pages. **Suggestion:** show it only to that player's team and staff, and on game line-ups. Your call.
5. **"Womxn's"**: CTFDA's local leagues use "Womxn's"; WFDF divisions use "Women's". The app says "Women". **Suggestion:** show "Womxn's" for CTFDA leagues.
6. **Under-18s:** the Code of Conduct binds parents of minor members, and the form must be completed by a parent or guardian. The app shows the guardian note but can't verify it. **Ask CTFDA** whether that's enough, or whether a guardian's name and number are needed.
7. **Who decides fees and approves leagues** is the CTFDA committee (6a). In the app that maps to admins (fees, links, lists) and coordinators (leagues). **Agree with CTFDA who gets which level.**
8. **SAFDA documents:** SAFDA's site has no constitution, code of conduct or privacy policy. The Inclusion Guidelines note SAFDA has no transgender policy. **Ask SAFDA** for whatever exists before national sign-up goes through the app.
9. **Data agreement:** before importing past CTFDA forms, CTFDA should agree in writing that the app processes members' data on its behalf (POPIA "operator" agreement). See `MUST-DO.md`.
